ABOUT NEUROATLAS

A map, not a mind reader.

NeuroAtlas is designed to teach how brain systems interact. The colors represent a qualitative model of relative engagement under an educational scenario. They are not fMRI readings and should never be interpreted as a diagnosis or a measurement of a person's actual brain activity.
