About NeuroAtlas

Explore how brain systems interact.

NeuroAtlas combines anatomical exploration with qualitative educational models of emotion, networks, scenarios and meditation. The state colors describe functional emphasis in the selected model; they are not measurements of a person's brain activity.

The anatomical mesh is provided by the open Brain Project, whose source-derived 3-D assets are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. NeuroAtlas should not be used for diagnosis or clinical decisions.
