# Anatomy attribution

NeuroAtlas uses the open 3-D anatomical model from the Brain Project:
https://github.com/itayinbarr/brainproject

The Brain Project README states that its 3-D anatomy assets are derived from Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D and related open imaging atlases and are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution to Z-Anatomy contributors and BodyParts3D / DBCLS.

When distributing NeuroAtlas with this anatomy asset, retain the applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and license notice.
